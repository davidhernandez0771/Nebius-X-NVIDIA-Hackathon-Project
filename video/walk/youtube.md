## Title

SANT: 10 human demos in, a tested robot out (Nebius x NVIDIA Hackathon)

## Description

SANT (Simulate, Augment, Neaten, and Train) turns a few quick, imperfect human demos into a robot that works.
Every demo is replayed in NVIDIA Isaac Lab, repaired where physics says no, and multiplied into verified copies.
NVIDIA Nemotron, on Nebius Token Factory, proposes; physics verifies.

The result: a camera policy trained from 10 mouse demos succeeded on 30 of 40 unseen, pre-registered scenes
(95% Wilson interval 60-86%). Copying the raw demos directly: 0 of 40.

All robot footage is simulation (NVIDIA Isaac Lab, a Franka arm); no physical robot was used. The narration is an AI voice.
