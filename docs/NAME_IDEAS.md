# Project name ideas

Working title in the README is `[Project Name]`. Ten options, each with a
one-line pitch. Check availability of the name (domain, repo, trademark) before
committing to one; none of these have been checked.

| # | Name | Pitch |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Tidy Room** | Photograph a shelf, confirm what's on it, and let Nemotron suggest a neater layout. |
| 2 | **ShelfSense** | A vision model reads your shelf; you decide what it got right. |
| 3 | **Stow** | Know where everything is, and where it should go. |
| 4 | **Nook** | The small AI that keeps every drawer and corner of your room in order. |
| 5 | **Declutter Copilot** | An assistant that proposes, and you approve every change. |
| 6 | **Findr** | Ask "where's the lamp?" in plain language and get the shelf it lives on. |
| 7 | **Roomwise** | Your room's inventory, built from photos and confirmed by you. |
| 8 | **Second Shelf** | A second pair of eyes that sorts what you own into keep, unsure, and toss. |
| 9 | **Keepsort** | Sort every item into organize, unknown, or trash, one photo at a time. |
| 10 | **Nemo Nest** | Nemotron-powered home organizing, with a nod to the model behind it. |

## Suggestions

- **Best fit for the design rule** (the AI only proposes, you decide): *Declutter
  Copilot* or *Keepsort*.
- **Shortest and most brandable:** *Stow* or *Nook*.
- **Most obvious to hackathon judges:** *Nemo Nest*, since it points at
  Nemotron, though it can read as a Finding Nemo reference.
