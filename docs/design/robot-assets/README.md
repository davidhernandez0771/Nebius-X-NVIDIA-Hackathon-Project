# Robot assets: what the meshes need in Blender

The downloads are in `C:\dev\assets\robots` (sources, commits and licences in its `SOURCES.md`). This folder holds
what came out of them:

- `manifest.json`: per robot, the arm chain (joints, axes, limits), the meshes per link with their offsets, scales,
  formats, triangle counts and sizes, a rest pose with its source, the DoF and the reach;
- `blender.md`: the same meshes as tables, per robot and per file.

Both are generated by `uv run python scripts/robot_asset_manifest.py`, which also writes the catalog panel's
`web/src/world/stations/robots/specs.json`. Nothing is in the repo but these numbers: the meshes stay in
`C:\dev\assets`.

## The wall's budget

The brief asks for about 50–150k triangles on the wall. That's ~15k per arm, 105k for all seven. Today the arms
are 76k to 1.0M triangles each:

| Robot | Triangles | Decimate (Collapse) ratio for ~15k |
|---|---|---|
| Franka Panda (FER) | 134,924 | 0.11 |
| Franka Research 3 | 240,624 | 0.062 |
| UR5e | 121,789 | 0.12 |
| xArm 6 + xArm gripper (base only) | 75,650 | 0.20 |
| SO-101 | 398,884 | 0.038 |
| PiPER | 183,746 | 0.082 |
| OpenArm v1.0 (one arm + gripper) | 1,006,467 | 0.015 |

**Where to spend the budget:**
- Decimate per link, not per arm: the long links (upper arm, forearm) take the cut, and the small, curved ones (wrist
  caps, fingers) keep more.
- A Planar decimate first (angle ~5°) removes flat-face triangles before Collapse; try it on the heaviest (SO-101,
  PiPER, OpenArm).
- Then **Weighted Normals** and auto smooth, so the decimated links don't look faceted under the picture lights.

## Per robot

- **Franka Panda (FER) and FR3** (`franka/meshes/robots/{fer,fr3}/visual/link0-7.dae`, the white hand in
  `meshes/robot_ee/franka_hand_white/visual/`):
  - Collada in metres, Z-up: Blender's Collada importer gets the scale and the axes right.
  - The links carry their materials. Merge or replace them with the wall's two (paint and dark) to keep draw calls
    down.
  - The right finger is the left one turned π about z (the hand's xacro).
  - The FER is the Panda SANT runs in Isaac. The FR3's meshes are heavier (240k) for the same chain.
- **UR5e** (`ur5e/meshes/ur5e/visual/*.dae`):
  - Collada in metres, Z-up.
  - Each mesh has an offset in `config/ur5e/visual_parameters.yaml` (the manifest's `origin`: the base and shoulder
    turned 180° about z; the upper arm and forearm lifted 0.138 / 0.007 m and turned). Apply it, or the links sit
    off their joints.
  - Use the BSD-3 `ur5e` meshes only. The UR15/18/20/30/8long meshes have a restrictive licence and weren't
    downloaded.
- **xArm 6** (`xarm6/xarm_description/meshes/xarm6/visual/*.stl`, the gripper in `meshes/gripper/xarm/`):
  - STL with no units: the URDF uses scale 1, so they are metres.
  - No materials: use the wall's paint and dark.
  - The gripper's base sits on link6's flange. Its fingers and knuckles are posed by the gripper's own linkage xacro,
    which isn't resolved here: pose them by eye, or leave the gripper closed.
- **SO-101** (`so101/Simulation/SO101/assets/*.stl`):
  - STL in metres (URDF scale 1).
  - 17 meshes: printed parts plus servo bodies.
  - The heaviest arm after OpenArm (399k): check the servo bodies keep their shape after decimating.
  - The `.part` files beside them aren't referenced by the URDF: not needed.
- **PiPER** (`piper/src/piper_description/meshes/*.STL`):
  - STL in metres (URDF scale 1).
  - Upper-case `.STL`: Blender doesn't mind, but a web loader matching `.stl` would.
  - No materials.
- **OpenArm v1.0** (`openarm/assets/robot/openarm_v1.0/mesh/arm/visual/*.dae`, the gripper in
  `openarm/assets/end_effector/parallel_link/meshes/visual/`):
  - **Scale:** the Collada files say `unit meter="1"`, but the URDF scales them by **0.001**: they are in
    millimetres. Import, then scale by 0.001, or the arm is 1000× too big.
  - **Mirrored finger:** the left finger uses scale `0.001 -0.001 0.001`, a mirror. Apply the scale and recalculate
    normals (Shift+N), or it renders inside out.
  - **Size:** 1.0M triangles, 68 MB. Decimate before anything else; ratio ≈ 0.015 for the wall.
  - **The one arm:** the manifest takes the right arm of the generated bimanual example (`urdf/example/v1.urdf`),
    which is the unimanual OpenArm SANT ran in Isaac. The torso meshes weren't downloaded.

## Rest poses

| Robot | Rest pose | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Franka Panda | Isaac Lab's start pose | `web/src/twin/model.ts`: SANT's replay start |
| UR5e | `config/initial_positions.yaml` | its repo |
| FR3, xArm 6, SO-101, PiPER, OpenArm | each joint at the value nearest 0 within its limits | none in their repos |

For display, pose them by eye in Blender. The manifest's rest values are a starting point, not a design.

## Specs (for the catalog)

**Reach**, for every arm: the largest distance from the shoulder (the frame of the arm's 2nd joint) to the flange
over the joint limits, computed from each chain (`sim_worker/robot_assets.py`).

It matches what SANT measured:
- Franka 0.858 m (T2: "≈ 0.85 m");
- OpenArm 0.438 m (T2: "shoulder to wrist ≤ 0.44 m").

Vendors' brochures measure differently (UR quotes 850 mm for the UR5e; this gives 0.969 m), so only compare
reaches made this way.

**Payload:** no repo states one, so none is shown.
