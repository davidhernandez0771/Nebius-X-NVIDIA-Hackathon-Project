## Decisions

1. **Quaternion boundary.** Isaac Lab 3.0 uses **xyzw** for every config `rot`, every `*_pose_w` and the IK command (see `isaaclab/utils/math.py` and the tutorial's `(x,y,z,qx,qy,qz,qw)` goals). `replay.py` converts to and from the contract's wxyz at the boundary only.
2. **`ee_pos` is the tool-center point:** `panda_hand` + 0.1034 m along hand +z. That puts the fingertip center at the cube center when the gripper closes. `ee_quat` is the hand orientation.
3. **Contact sensors are on the counterparts, not on each arm link.**
   - Why: PhysX filtered contacts need one sensing body per env.
   - Layout:
     - the cube is filtered against the two fingers, which gives `contact_gripper_cube`;
     - the table and each of the 5 bin parts are filtered against the robot links (link1–7, hand and fingers; link0 is excluded), which gives `contact_arm_table`, `contact_arm_bin` and `max_contact_force`.
   - Each sensor uses `history_length=2`, so both physics substeps count.
   - The contact threshold is 0.1 N.
4. **Prim paths.**
   - Filter patterns are globs, not regexes: `(Geometry/.*/)?` matched nothing (exact error below).
   - The 3.0 Franka bodies are nested: `Robot/Geometry/panda_link0/panda_link1/…/panda_hand/panda_leftfinger`. `scene_cfg.py` spells them out.
5. **Table and bin are kinematic rigid cuboids.**
   - Kinematic bodies can carry contact sensors and can be moved per Scene.
   - The table top is at z = 0 and starts at x = 0.15, so the robot base never touches it.
   - Bin frame origin = the center of the floor's underside. The floor is 0.01 m thick, the walls rise to z = 0.09 (`BIN_RIM_Z`), and the inner box is 0.20 × 0.20 × 0.08.
6. **Pre-roll.**
   - Before recording, the arm is driven to the trajectory's first pose for up to 200 control steps; it stops at a 3 mm residual, which took 38 steps here.
   - Recorded step k = the state after commanding step k. Physics dt is 0.01 with decimation 2, and IK is recomputed on every physics substep (as in the tutorial).
7. **Gripper control.** `panda_finger_joint1` and `panda_finger_joint2` both get the target (0.04 open, 0.0 closed); joint2 has no drive in the 3.0 USD (it follows by mimic). `action` = the full 9-joint target vector.
8. **Scripted expert.**
   - "0.15 m/s" means the average speed of each minimum-jerk move (peak 0.28 m/s), with a 0.4 s minimum per move.
   - The cube yaw is wrapped to ±45° because the cube looks the same every 90°.
   - The hand returns to `(0,1,0,0)` during the lift.
   - Each gripper value takes effect at the sample where the hand arrives, so close and open events land exactly on the cube center and on the release point.
9. **Release point** = bin origin + 0.10 m (the plan's "bin floor + 0.10"), which is about 1 cm above the rim.
10. **`check_success` details.**
    - `path_length` uses the trajectory's (commanded) `ee_pos`.
    - `max_contact_force` = the maximum of the SimSignals array.
    - A drop = contact lost while `gripper == 1` and the cube is outside the bin's xy footprint (the footprint check respects bin yaw).
11. **One sim session per process.** `replay()` builds the SimulationContext and scene on first use and reuses them; it must run inside `launch_simulation(make_sim_cfg(...), args)`. A session built without cameras raises if `record_cameras=True` is asked for later.
12. **Printing.** Leaving `launch_simulation` ends the process, so the smoke script prints its results inside the `with` block.
13. **`TASK_TEXT` is defined in `replay.py`,** because T1's `rules.py` is not on this branch. The lead should dedupe it at integration.

## 2026-10-16 — Task 2: IK lag fixed (measured) → joint velocity feedforward is the default

**Root cause (measured, not guessed).** The lag is the joint PD drive, not IK convergence.
`FRANKA_PANDA_HIGH_PD_CFG` has stiffness 400 and damping 80, and replay sent position targets only (velocity target 0), so the damping term pulls against every moving target.
A PD drive following a ramp lags by about damping / stiffness = 0.2 s. At the 0.28 m/s minimum-jerk peak that predicts ≈ 56 mm; sprint 1 measured a 51 mm max.
Confirmations:
- More IK iterations change nothing (26.8 → 26.7 mm).
- Sending the commanded twist as joint velocity targets removes 92% of the error.

**Setup.**
- Scenes: 20 `sample_scene(default_rng(seed), "franka", seed)`, seeds 0–19.
