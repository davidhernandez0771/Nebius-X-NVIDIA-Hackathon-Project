## Conflicts and how they were solved

- **A. `tests/test_dp_robota.py::test_copied_sim_constants_match_sim_worker` failed after #3 (semantic, no
  conflict marker).** `pm/robota-export` kept its own copy of `CUBE_SIZE` and the bin's inner size, with a test
  that regex-reads them from `sim_worker/scene_cfg.py`. `pm/achieved-path` moved those constants out of
  `scene_cfg.py` into `demo_pipeline/scene_geometry.py` (`scene_cfg.py` now imports them), so the regex found
  nothing. Fix, as its own commit `7ce5bb1` (not inside the merge, so it's easy to review): `robota.py` imports
  `BIN_INNER` and `CUBE_SIZE` from `scene_geometry` (`BIN_INNER_XY = BIN_INNER[:2]`), and the test checks that
  `scene_cfg.py` imports the same module. Same values: a 0.05 m cube and a 0.20 × 0.20 m bin. The camera checks
  in that test are unchanged and pass.

Nothing else conflicted.
