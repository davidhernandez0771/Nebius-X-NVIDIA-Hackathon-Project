## How to view it

```
npm --prefix web run dev
```

Open **http://localhost:5173/robots**. Scroll moves through the pile, a click on a robot cartridge (or a name in
the list) picks it, a second click or Esc drops it. Touch drags scroll on phones.

- **Still shelf:** turn effects off (`/design` has the toggle, or set `localStorage["sant.effects"] = "off"`) or
  set the OS to reduced motion. You get a still 2D row of the same cartridges.
- **Side-by-side check:** `python -m http.server` in `docs/design/prototypes` serves the prototype.

### Fix 1: cartridges never intersect

- **Layout.** Each cartridge gets the prototype's jitter (tilt, reach, step), then is lifted in 0.02 steps until
  its oriented bounding box clears every cartridge below by 0.06. That leaves room for both neighbours'
  ±0.025 bob. A lift over 0.3 re-rolls the jitter (up to 6 tries) and keeps the lowest.
- **Cost.** The mean step went from 0.42 to 0.705, so the pile is taller and looser. That is the price of zero
  overlaps at these tilts.
- **Picking.** The prototype's slide-out also passed through neighbours (tested: 146 overlapping poses). The pile
  now parts while a robot slides out: above +1.0, below −0.7, scaled by how far out it is.
- **Tests:** no overlap at rest (all 435 pairs); along each robot's slide-out (every 0.05); and while switching
  between any two robots.

### Fix 2: key light toned down

- **Lights.** The main cause was r169's RoomEnvironment, far brighter than r128's: environment 0.9 → 0.2.
  Key spot 140 → 30, rim 3 → 2.5, glints 40/24 → 12/8.
- **Shapes and labels.** Rounded shells (radius 0.035) catch the thin edge highlights. Labels sit just under
  paper white with a softer clearcoat, so they stay readable.
- **Bloom.** HDR is capped at 2.2 before bloom (threshold 1.6, radius 0.2). A grazing rim glint became a white
  blob on phones; now it stays a small orb. Phones get half the bloom.
- **Grade.** Tone mapping happens in the grade pass after bloom, matching the prototype's grayscale look.

### The HUD

- **Kept exactly:** the darkened scene, the 3 tilted orbits with travelling dots, the title block, frame, "×"
  boxes, `x: y:` coordinates, light streaks, and the mosaic tiles in each robot's palette and effect.
- **Difference 1, fonts:** canvas text uses the site's Geist / Geist Mono (loaded in `web/index.html`) instead of
  Inter / IBM Plex Mono. It is one constant each in `fonts.ts` if the user wants the prototype's fonts back.
- **Difference 2, anchor:** the lock-on point is clamped so the frame and title stay on the stage. In the
  prototype the HUD ran off the right edge on phones, and off the bottom for Franka, which sits low in the pile.

## Perf pass (commit f7d5773, recovered after the PC crashed mid-task)

This work was uncommitted when the PC crashed. After the crash I re-ran the web tests and build (both pass,
above) and committed the work unchanged. **I did not look at it in the browser after the crash.**

- **Draw calls:** each cartridge part is one instanced mesh, so 30 cartridges take 4 draws. Picking uses an
  invisible instanced hit box. All labels share one atlas texture (`atlas.ts`).
- **Post:** `post.ts` replaces EffectComposer. It uses one half-float target. Bloom runs on a smaller copy,
  and its bright-pass also caps HDR. The grade draws straight to the canvas. The canvas has no MSAA, depth or
  stencil buffer.
- **Quality tiers** (`quality.ts`): three tiers. DPR goes 1.5 / 1 / 0.85 and bloom goes 0.5 / 0.35 / off.
  drei's `PerformanceMonitor` moves between them. Software GPUs start on the low tier.
- **Page:** wheel input is applied once per frame. Rendering stops while the tab is hidden or the stage is
  scrolled off-screen. The DPR cap went from 1.75 to 1.5.
- **HUD:** the mosaic tiles are captured once per selection or resize and then cached. They are no longer
  read back every frame.
- **Layout:** messier than before: wider tilts, a size per cartridge, and 5 copies that reach far out. It is
  still collision-free (the tests pass). `PART_UP` went from 1.0 to 1.3.
- **`?perf` overlay:** shows fps percentiles, a hitch log and renderer counters. Options: `&sweep` runs a
  scripted scroll (a cold pass, then a warm pass, with results in `window.__perfResult`). `&dpr=` and
  `&tier=0..2` pin the settings, and `&sync` adds the GPU wait to each frame's time.

What the perf pass did not do:

- **No measurement:** no before/after `?perf&sweep` numbers were recorded, so the speed-up is unmeasured.
- **No visual check:** nobody compared it to the prototype by eye after instancing, the new post chain and
  the messier layout.
- **No Isaac test:** it was not timed with Isaac sharing the GPU, which is the case it was built for.
