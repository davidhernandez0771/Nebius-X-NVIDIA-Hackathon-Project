# Polish, 2026-10-27 (branch `pm/polish`, from `pm/live-14`)

Six fixes, one commit each, never pushed or merged. Screenshots are in `2026-10-27-polish/` (`before-*` from
`pm/live-14`, `after-*` from this branch). They were taken in headless Chrome on SwiftShader only, at 1280×720 and
390×844 (the frame rate shown in them is SwiftShader's).

There is a before and after pair for each of these: `forest`, `reactor`, `ring`, `hubtags`, `proof`, `proof-2` and
`proof-3` (the proof panels at 1280×720), plus `after-ask-tour-1280x720.jpg`. `shots-after.json` has the draw
calls and the measured boxes.

## What changed

1. **The fireflies stay out of the meshes** (`d694f2c`; `intro/fireflyPlan.ts`, `fireflies.ts`, `forestRobots.ts`).
   - The trunks (near and far), the fallen logs and the robots are now solids: vertical cylinders, made by `solids()`.
   - Each firefly is fitted among them when the plan is made:
     - A drifting one's home is nudged off the trunks, and it wanders less in a tight spot.
     - A robot one's circle is moved clear of its robot. Where a trunk crosses the circle, the firefly swings back and forth along the clear stretch instead of going all the way round.
   - In the shader, `settle()` (mirrored on the CPU) pushes each firefly out of the 6 solids nearest its path and keeps it over the floor.
   - A firefly near a surface draws a smaller halo, so its glow doesn't cut into the bark.
   - The tests check every tier's fireflies against every body in the forest: none goes in, and none jumps more than about 5 cm in a frame.
   - It still makes one draw call.
   - Cost: making the plan takes about 77 ms on ultra (600 fireflies, in Node) and less on the lower tiers. It runs once, when the forest is built.
2. **The reactor's inside looks more realistic** (`d994c35`; `intro/Reactor.tsx`, shaders only).
   - Seen down THE RING, the core used to be a flat, milky disc.
   - The inside is now a steel liner: stacked rings, seams, and slots where the plasma shows.
   - The light grows stronger the deeper you look. Its glow isn't fogged, because the cavern's mist is outside the core.
   - At the bottom is a source disc: white-hot in the middle, teal at the rim, with slow swirls and rings of heat.
   - The collar, plinth, ribs and clamps now have brushed panels, seams, bolts and a highlight that moves with the camera.
   - The mouth of the well glows, and THE RING picks up the core's light on its underside.
   - Only the brand's teal and white are used: no green, no eye.
3. **The proof wall fits its screen at 1280×720** (`a31bae6`; `stations/proof/proof.css`).
   - The climb and teacher panels scrolled by 60 and 97 px.
   - Most of that came from the graphs' screen-reader tables. A table ignores `.world-sr`'s 1 px box, so the table hung below the panel. The tables are now pinned to the top corner, still clipped.
   - The data panels now put the head above the summary in the left column (a subgrid), so the graph uses the full height.
   - The links sit beside the steps, and short screens tighten the spacing.
   - Measured: no overflow at 1100×700, 1280×720, 1280×800, 1366×768, 1440×900 or 1920×1080. At 1024×640 one panel is still 4 px too tall and scrolls.
   - Phone and portrait layouts are unchanged.
4. **The room's tags read cleanly on a phone** (`7f39c0a`; `stations/workshop/workshop.css`).
   - Under 700 px wide, the five cards are now one-line chips: number and name, 40 px tall, with a 22 px stem.
   - They have a solid glass background instead of the blur, which smeared the cartridges behind them.
   - Desktop and the holographic labels are unchanged.
5. **Ask SANT's "start tutorial" opens the new tour** (`3752d29`).
   - `useAsk` now calls the guide's `requestGuide()`. In the room view the first card opens; from anywhere else the guide takes the visitor to the room view first.
   - The old camera tour's files (`tour.ts`, `TourCaption.tsx`) are kept but emptied, with a note. Nothing imports them.
   - The old tour's caption and CSS are gone.
   - The tool's description sent to Nemotron now names the guide's four cards.
   - New test: `ask/askTour.test.tsx`.
6. **The flaky DataPage test** (`e1c1f07`).
   - Every wait in "shows the variants…" is now a `findBy*` with an 8 s timeout, and the test has 20 s.
   - It still checks the same four things.
