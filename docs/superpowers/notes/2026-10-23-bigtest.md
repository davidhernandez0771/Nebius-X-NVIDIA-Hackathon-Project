**The gripper.**
- f = the mean of the student's two finger targets.
- Closed (0.0 m) when f < 15 mm; open (0.040 m) when f > 25 mm; in between, the last state holds. Every episode starts open.
- 0.25 is the teacher's deadband on its [-1, 1] output (demo_v6h params/env.yaml), mapped around the 20 mm midpoint.
- The training labels need nothing: the 10-22 @600 export has exactly two finger values, 0.0 m (151,300 frames) and 0.040 m (185,429 frames).
- Why it may matter: on the 10-22 practice trace (5200-5204, @300 s60000, 5 episodes only), the two time-limit failures held the fingers mid-range (8-32 mm) for 96/1500 and 702/1500 steps. The three successes did so for at most 4.

## Risks

- **Winner's curse:** best of 3 checkpoints on 29 seeds overstates; only the test count is the estimate (prereg).
- **Human repeats:** with few held-human passes, the human repeat factor grows (the floor is 80).
- **DAgger untested at scale:** cap_from_tool has run only in unit tests, never in a full round.
- **One combined arm:** no single change gets the credit.
- **Reused rollouts:** they were recorded at 95663cb; round.json's rollouts_code says so.
