## How the scenes are picked

`build_look_clip.py` keeps every seed that meets all three conditions:
- both policies have a trace;
- the raw policy failed;
- v2 succeeded.

The raw policy failed all 40 of its counted runs, so in effect this keeps v2's successes. **It's a cherry-pick for the
picture, and it's labelled:**
- the kicker reads "Illustrative replay · picked to show the difference · seed N";
- "How these runs were picked" shows the rule from `index.json` `pick`, and where each run stands (below);
- it links to the test track's 40 runs, failures included.

`--seeds` picks and orders by hand, and refuses any seed that isn't a raw failure and a v2 success. With option (B),
5403 opens first. If the raw robot looks too still on one of them, put a livelier one first.

## The builder (`scripts/build_look_clip.py`, `tests/test_build_look_clip.py`)

- It reuses `build_test_track.build_final_test`, which got a `seeds=` filter (only the picked episodes are written).
  - So every trace is re-judged by SANT's `check_success` against the bin re-made from its seed, and has to match its log.
- **The caption's numbers** are read from the two counted evals' own files (`raw_10.json`: 0/40; `bigtest_final.json`: 30/40), each checked against its episode log.
- **It refuses** traces when:
  - they come from another checkpoint than the headline's;
  - they ran with other inference options (v2's deadband 0.25, raw's none);
  - the two sides start from different arm poses;
  - the two sides' traces start with a different cube or bin.
- **Each side's standing:**

  | Standing | Meaning |
  |---|---|
  | `counted` | One of the headline's own runs. |
  | `rerun` | A traced re-run of a counted seed that ended exactly as the counted run did. If it ended differently, the build stops. |
  | `illustrative` | Run for this clip on a seed its headline didn't count. |
- **Writes** `web/public/look-clip/index.json` and `runs/{raw,v2}/<seed>.json`, in the test track's run format at 50 Hz.
- It never deletes old run files; `index.json` names the ones in use.

## The site (`web/src/site/lookclip/`)

- **`LookClip`** is the embeddable component (`import { LookClip } from "…/site/lookclip/LookClip"`).
  - Two `RobotTwin`s on one clock (`useTrackClock`, moved out of `TestTrackPage` into `testtrack/useTrackClock.ts`), with one shared camera framing.
  - **What it shows:**
    - the caption;
    - each side's title and its pre-registered count with its seeds;
    - "Running · t s", until the quicker robot finishes. Then both verdicts show: "✓ In the bin · 7.5 s · verified by SANT" and "✕ Failed · never touched the cube · kept trying to the 30 s limit". The slower robot's verdict is for its whole run.
  - **Clip length:** it plays until the quicker run ends plus 2.5 s, then holds 2 s on the last frame and loops.
  - **Props:**
    - `headingLevel` (2 or 3);
    - `seed`;
    - `onSeed`;
    - `interactive`: off by default, so an embed never catches the page's scroll.
  - **Pausing:** it pauses when off screen. With reduced motion it rests on the result frame.
  - **Without its data:** it shows "The side-by-side replay didn't load" and a link to `/test-track`.
- **`/look`** (`LookClipPage`):
  - the clip alone in the holo frame;
  - `?seed=` picks the pair, and the scene buttons keep it in the URL;
  - `?embed=1` drops the frame, for an iframe or the mp4 screen capture.
- **Phones:** the robots stay side by side in taller arenas. There was no horizontal scroll at 390 px.
