## The bugs, their causes, the fixes

| # | What the user saw | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | "LOADING THE ROBOT WALL 3.2 / 3.2 MB" never went away | The in-place loading state (`ui/Wait.tsx`) waits for the place's files **and** for its scene to stand (`core/standing.ts`). The robot wall has no Scene of its own (it is part of the workshop's), so "robots" never stood. | `core/waiting.ts` `standKey()`: the wall stands with the workshop. |
| 2 | The same over the machine's projection (the zoom) | The zoom has no Scene either (it plays out of the machine's). | `standKey("zoom")` = the machine. |
| 1b | No robot close-up | There never was one: a click on a robot only lit its catalog entry. | New: a click on a robot (or its entry) flies the camera to a close-up of it (`stations/robots/index.tsx` `closeUp`, the station's `shot`), clear of the panel (16:9) or above the sheet (phones); "All robots" or Esc steps back to the wall. |
| 3 | Tab away and back: the room stays dark | `Pause` (`core/Perf.tsx`) stopped frames with r3f's `setFrameloop`, which **restarts the canvas clock at 0**. Everything timed on it was put back before its own start: the lamps' power-on (`Lamps.tsx`) read a negative time and held the lights OFF until the clock caught up again (as long as the visit so far). | `createPause`: the clock keeps its time across the pause. A restored WebGL context asks for a frame. |
| 4a | From the shelf, STORY / DESCENT broke the camera | The journey rail never let go of the held shot (`store.override`): the camera kept the shelf's shot over the story. | Any rail jump, any branch opening, lets go of the held shot (`WorldRoot` `goTo` / `jump`); the shelf, a detail view and the tour end with it (`stepper.ts` `followRelease`). |
| 4b | After leaving the shelf the page wouldn't scroll | Two causes: the shelf stayed "open" underneath, so its wheel handler kept eating the wheel; and **four** effects (the room view's, the held shot's, the branch's and the loader's) each saved the page's `overflow` and put "their" old value back, so a stale `hidden` outlived what set it. | One owner: `core/pageHold.ts` `pageHeld()` (loading, a branch, a held shot, or the landed room view) applied by one effect in `WorldRoot`, which writes whenever the page differs from it. |
| 4c | Shelf → machine → back: all UI gone | Back returned to the shelf's held shot; the room's UI only shows in the room view (no held shot). | Opening a branch lets go of the shelf: Back lands in the room view, UI and all. |
| 5 | No clear way out | Small inline links, different per place. | `ui/StationBar.tsx`: a real "← Back to room" button, top centre (where the hub bar sits), on every place you can enter (the shelf, the machine, the projection, the bench, the robot wall), with where you are, one line on what to do there and **Esc**. The hub bar now says "Click a tag to go in · Esc comes back out". |
| 6 | Dashed glowing rings under the lamps, flickering, ultra only | The light shafts (`scene/Haze.tsx`, ultra only) took `pow(1.0 - vDown, 1.6)`: at the rim the interpolated `vDown` runs a hair past 1, and `pow()` of a negative base is undefined (NaN on D3D); the bloom spread the NaN pixels into dashes. | The base is clamped. The shafts also end in the air above the floor, and a soft analytic pool of light lies under each lamp on every tier (`scene/FloorPools.tsx`, `lightPools.ts`: one draw call, additive, no depth write, polygon offset, dithered). |

## What the user should check

1. **The robot wall:** no "Loading the robot wall" once it is in. Click a robot on the wall (or its name in the list):
   the camera closes in on it. "All robots" or Esc goes back to the wall; "Back to room" or Esc again goes back to the
   room.
2. **The machine → "Into the projection":** no "Loading the machine" over the dolly.
3. **Tab away for a while, come back:** the room is lit at once.
4. **The shelf, then the journey rail's Story or Descent:** the story plays from the top (the camera isn't stuck on the
   shelf) and the page scrolls. Also: shelf → a robot cartridge (or Plug in) → machine → "Back to room" lands in the
   room view with its tags and hub bar.
5. **The way out:** a "← Back to room" button top centre at the shelf, the machine, the projection, the bench and the
   robot wall, with Esc. On a phone it is two lines (the buttons, then the hint) and Contact steps down under it.
6. **The floor under the two lamps, on each tier** (the quality chip, bottom right): a soft warm pool, no dashed
   rings, nothing flickering as the mouse turns the view.
