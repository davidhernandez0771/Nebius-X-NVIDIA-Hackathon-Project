> Ten sloppy mouse demos became a robot that succeeds 30 times out of 40 on scenes it has never seen, while copying the
> same demos directly succeeded 0 times. SANT is what makes cheap human demos usable: it checks them in physics,

judge records a demo, watches SANT check and multiply it, then sees what that data produced: the side-by-side and the
test track's 40 real runs. The cube task is the benchmark that proves the method, not the product.
