## Tonight's checklist

### 0. The setup

- The Kit window looks from **behind the robot**, over its right shoulder.
- /record's chips show `keyboard · relay · sim` once you start. No "relay rejected a message".

### 1. Keyboard (the default; from [keyboard-mode](2026-10-19-keyboard-mode.md))

1. Keyboard → **Start keyboard**.
2. **Directions** (watch the Kit window and the twin):
   - W = away from the base, S = toward it;
   - D = the robot's (and your) right, A = left;
   - R = up, F = down.
3. **Smoothness:** tap and hold keys. The arm glides in and out, never jerks. Shift = fine moves near the cube, and
   try the Speed slider at 6–12 cm/s for grasps.
4. **Space** closes and opens the gripper. Do a full pick-and-place: approach, F down to the cube, Space, R up, move
   over the bin, Space.
5. **Enter** starts a recording; Enter again stops and saves. `demo.json` has `"input": "keyboard"`.
6. Typing in any field, or the arrows on a focused slider, must not move the arm.

### 2. Mouse (never verified; from [keyboard-mode](2026-10-19-keyboard-mode.md) and [mouse-click](2026-10-20-mouse-click-and-camera-video.md))

1. **Click to take the mouse.** Pick Mouse and *don't* press Start. Click the middle of the 3D twin: the cursor
   vanishes, the chips go to `mouse · Esc releases`, and moving the mouse moves the arm.
2. **The mapping:** mouse up = forward, right = the robot's right, wheel = up/down, click = gripper. Esc releases.
3. **Click again after Esc.** Press Esc, then click again at once: you should see the "needs a second after Esc"
   line. Wait a second and click again: it locks.
4. **Every view takes the mouse:** the side, top and align views.
5. **No orbit:** dragging on the twin doesn't rotate it in Mouse mode. Back in Keyboard, it orbits again.
6. **Sensitivity:** try the slider. If one setting feels good, it can become the default.
7. **The label:** a saved mouse demo has `"input": "mouse"`.

### 3. Contact glow and soft floor (from [record-contact](2026-10-20-record-contact.md), in this order)

1. **Which link glows** (the one real unknown).
   - Lower the open gripper onto the table and touch it lightly. The fingers (or the hand) glow amber, and the bar
     reads e.g. "1.2 N · left finger on the table".
   - Then push one forearm link against a bin wall: that link, not another, glows.
   - If the wrong link glows, the sensor's column order differs (fix in `read_contact`). The forces are still right.
2. **Colours:** a light touch is amber, and pushing harder turns red past 5 N. The bar's 5 N tick is about 30% along.
3. **Soft floor in the bin** (keyboard or mouse).
   - Soft floor on: drive over the bin and hold F / wheel down.
   - The arm stops with ee_target_z ≈ 0.08, "soft floor holding" shows, and the peak stays small (no bin-floor
     touch).
4. **Placing still works.**
   - Pick the cube, carry it over the bin, lower until the floor holds, release. The cube drops ~4–5 cm onto the bin
     floor.
   - The verifier on the saved demo does not fail with `high_release`.
   - `demo.json` has `"assist": {"soft_floor": true, "soft_floor_steps": >0}`.
5. **Camera mode:** push the hand down over the bin. It stops at the rim the same way.
6. **Off:** untick "soft floor", then lower into the bin.
   - The arm reaches down, and the bar goes red if it presses.
   - The demo has `"assist": {"soft_floor": false, "soft_floor_steps": 0}`.
7. **The table:** with the default calibration the target never goes below 0.02, so the table floor shouldn't
   engage. A link pressed on the table still glows and counts in the peak.
8. **Cost:** with the latency trace on (`?latency=1`), the sink's `contact` section is well under 1 ms. Say if
   teleop feels slower with /record open.
9. **Peak:** after a take, "peak N N" matches the largest force you saw. A demo pressed like last night's reads
   hundreds of N; one placed with the floor on reads small.

### 4. Camera mode (from [camera-fixes](2026-10-19-camera-fixes.md) and [camera video](2026-10-20-mouse-click-and-camera-video.md))

1. **Pinch 4× while still:** the arm's depth (the ee_x tile, the twin) stays put, about 1 cm at most. If not, take a
   Debug trace (Settings → Debug trace, 10 s).
2. **Right is right:** moving your hand right moves the arm to its right; toward the camera reaches forward. If it's
   mirrored, untick "Robot's view" and report it.
3. **Clutch:** reach far right, hold Shift, bring your hand back to the middle, let go. The arm stays, then follows
   from there.
4. **Sensitivity 1.5–2×:** smaller hand motions, same reach. Moving the slider doesn't move the arm.
5. **Spacebar gripper** (Settings → Gripper: spacebar): Space closes and opens, a pinch does nothing, and the depth
   doesn't move on Space.
6. **Camera video:**
   - Record a short take. The foot says "camera video saved: demos/human_…/camera.webm"; play it.
   - Pick a pinch, find its `t_ms` in camera.json and seek to its `video_s`: the pinch is on screen within about a
     frame.
   - Stop & discard leaves no camera.webm. With the setting off, there's no camera.webm either.
   - The camera fps (the foot) doesn't drop noticeably with the setting on. A 1-minute take is a few MB.
