# Sprint 9 T5: the landing page (`/site`)

Branch `pm/web-landing`, from `origin/perfect-then-multiply` at `e94d817`. Not pushed.

## How to view it

```
npm --prefix web run dev        # then open http://localhost:5173/site
```

- **Effects off** (the switch in the nav) or the OS's reduced motion shows the still versions.
- **"Try it" / "Power on"** plays the dust power-on into `/pm/record`.
- **The hero takes about 8 s** to grow its swarm to full (1 → 4 → 16 → 64 → 256). A background or occluded
  browser window throttles `requestAnimationFrame` (measured at 1.5 fps here), so look at it in a focused
  window.

## What's on the page

The copy comes from T1's content spec (`docs/design/content-spec.md` §3, `deb5dfb` on `pm/web-review`), beat by
beat, with each number's source printed beside it. The beats are hero, problem, how SANT works, proof, bring
your robot, Nemotron, try it, then the footer. All copy is in `web/src/site/landing/copy.ts`.

- **Hero** (`hero/`): our take on Linear's "Animation Principle", studied frame by frame on recent.design.
  - **The hand:** a heavy ink hand built from the 21 hand-tracking landmarks, seen from the thumb side. It
    drifts slowly and pinches every 2.4 s.
  - **The swarm:** small robot-gripper marks repeat each pinch a moment later and multiply ×4 per pinch.
    - They sit on a stepped lattice fanning out from the pinch point. Far copies are blurred and fainter, for
      depth.
    - A newborn flashes teal, and a teal ripple runs out through the full swarm on each pinch.
    - Copies that drift behind the hero text fade to 12%, so the text stays readable at any width.
- **"A demo becomes data"** (`matrix/`): our take on the Vercel Store dot-matrix shader.
  - **The picture:** a frame from the `s1_0002` replay sheet (`docs/media`) dissolves into square ink dots sized
    by darkness. The cube's cells take the accent, and a few cells twinkle.
  - **The seam:** ragged and wandering. It follows the scroll through "How SANT works", where the figure is
    sticky beside the three steps.
- **Proof:** the foundation's `CountUpTiles` (7.5% → 42.5%, "same 10 demos: 3/40 → 17/40") with `ResultBars` for
  all three arms (R1, R2, R16) and the "What it does not show yet" box (C1–C4), always visible.
- **Bring your robot:** a static three-cartridge teaser (OpenArm slid out with "Design flaw found"), linking to
  `/robots`.
- **Try it:** the dust power-on, plus the existing pinch sheet (`web/src/landing/pinchSheet`, imported, not
  edited).

## The logo is now a swappable symbol

- **All of it lives in `web/src/sant/components/logoSymbol.ts`.** It holds a `viewBox` and one path per part,
  in lighting order.
- **The placeholder** follows logo-brief direction A: one larger original (accent) and three equal copies
  stepping up and to the right, never radial.
- **Where it shows:**
  - `Logo`, as the symbol plus the "SANT" wordmark; `wordmark={false}` gives the symbol alone;
  - both navs and the story footer;
  - the dust power-on, where the dust now gathers into the symbol's parts (sampled from the paths with
    `Path2D`), not into the letters.
- **To swap in the user's drawing:** paste its viewBox and path data into that file. Any number of parts works;
  the four power-on light steps spread over them.
