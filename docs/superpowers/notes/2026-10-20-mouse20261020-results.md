## 2. Align, multiply

- **Align:** every training episode starts at home: a min-jerk lead-in of k = 20 steps is put in front.
  - Raw: 10/10 start-aligned episodes verify.
  - Repaired: 10/10 start-aligned episodes verify.
- **Multiply:** 10 variants per repaired demo in new scenes (seeds 3100 + 100·i + k). **100/100 verified, yield 1.0.**
  Every demo got 10/10.
- **Grid** (15:37:05):
  - `raw_10`: 10 episodes. All 10 are the start-aligned recordings, and all 10 verify.
  - `perfected_multiply_10`: 110 episodes, the 10 repaired + 100 variants.
- **Timing:** align 15:08:36–~15:13; multiply until 15:37:04, about 14 s per variant. The sim stage took 32 min for
  all of it. That is within the pre-registered estimate's formula (1 + 1.4·10 + 2.6·10 = 41 min).
