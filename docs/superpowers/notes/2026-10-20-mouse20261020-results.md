## 2. Align, multiply

- **Align:** every training episode starts at home: a min-jerk lead-in of k = 20 steps is put in front.
  - Raw: 10/10 start-aligned episodes verify.
  - Repaired: 10/10 start-aligned episodes verify.
- **Multiply:** 10 variants per repaired demo in new scenes (seeds 3100 + 100·i + k). **100/100 verified, yield 1.0.**
  Every demo got 10/10.
- **Grid** (15:37:05):
  - `raw_10`: 10 episodes. All 10 are the start-aligned recordings, and all 10 verify.
  - `perfected_multiply_10`: 110 episodes, the 10 repaired + 100 variants.
- **Timing:** align 15:08:36–~15:13; multiply until 15:37:04, about 14 s per variant. The sim stage took 32 min for
  all of it. That is within the pre-registered estimate's formula (1 + 1.4·10 + 2.6·10 = 41 min).

## 4. Eval and result (pre-registered: two-sided Fisher, α = 0.05, Wilson 95% CIs)

- **Eval:** 40 unseen scenes, seeds 5020–5059. Both cells start from the raw dataset's `start_state.json`
  (`--start-pose dataset`). 17:46:29–19:19:41, under `gpu.lock`. All 40 episodes ran in each cell, with no errors.

| Cell | Successes | Rate | Wilson 95% CI | Failure reasons (an episode can have several) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `raw_10` | 0/40 | 0.0% | 0.0–8.8% | not_in_bin 40, collision 2 |
| `perfected_multiply_10` | 3/40 | 7.5% | 2.6–19.9% | not_in_bin 37, collision 27, dropped 26, gripper_closed 10 |

- **Result:** raw 0/40 vs SANT 3/40, two-sided Fisher **p = 0.24**. **Not significant** at α = 0.05. The
  pre-registered test does not show that SANT's data trained a better policy here.
- **The SANT successes:** seeds 5030, 5044 and 5052, each clean (no failure reason at all).
- **How the two policies fail (descriptive, not a tested claim):**
  - **Raw:** all 40 episodes ran to the time limit with the cube not in the bin. It recorded 0 drops: it never lost
    the cube from closed fingers short of the bin.
  - **SANT:** it gets the cube into its fingers.
    - 26/40 episodes record a drop: the fingers lost the cube while closed and not over the bin.
    - Plus the 3 successes.
  - **SANT collides a lot:** 27/40 episodes pushed the arm into the bin or table above 5 N. Raw did that 2/40 times.
  - **SANT ended 10/40 episodes with the gripper still closed.**
- **Open question, not concluded:** whether the soft floor taught SANT's policy to push down.
  - 9 of the 10 demos had the soft floor holding the target up (section 1), and the variants are built from those paths.
  - T6 is investigating this. Nothing here tests it.
- **Compared with the webcam run (`human10c`):** that run was 3/40 multiplied-only, with drops dominating. This is
  also 3/40, now with a raw arm at 0/40 beside it.
- **Caveats:**
  - 10 demos from one person in one session.
  - One training run per arm.
  - n = 40 per arm can only detect large differences: a 0/40 vs 3/40 split is well inside chance.
