## Task 1: Secrets across all history

Two stdlib scanners were written to the session scratchpad, not the repo. They read
`git log -p --all --unified=0` (added lines only) and the working tree.

- **`scan_secrets.py`** covers these patterns:
  - keys and tokens: `sk-`, `sk-ant-`, `ghp_/gho_/ghu_/ghs_/ghr_`, `github_pat_`, `eyJ…`
    three-part JWTs (the Nebius key shape), AWS `AKIA/ASIA` and secret-key assignments,
    Google `AIza`, Slack `xox?-`, HF `hf_`;
  - PEM private keys;
  - `*API_KEY|SECRET|TOKEN|PASSWORD = <literal ≥12 chars>`, `Authorization: Bearer`;
  - URLs with embedded credentials;

    trycloudflare).

  classes with Shannon entropy > 4.3.
