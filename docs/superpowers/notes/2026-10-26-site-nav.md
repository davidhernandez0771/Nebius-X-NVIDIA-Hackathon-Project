in-app link to `/` (Record's way back) fell back to the stills. The walk scripts put a "high" GPU in the session's
cached check (`world.gpu`) before each load, as a real machine would have. Without that the stills are what you
get, which is correct behaviour, not a bug.

## Where "back" goes, and why

**Top left, under (or right after) the SANT mark, everywhere.**
- **In the world:** under the floating mark, as a strip: the button, then where you are and what to do here, then
  `Esc goes back`. A step inside a place (a robot's close-up, a cartridge's detail) puts its own step first
  ("← All robots", "← Back to shelf").
- **On the 2D pages:** the first thing after the mark in the top strip.

**Why there:**
- **The eye.** Every place's own panel is on the left: the machine's caption and button bottom left, the bench's
  editor, the shelf's dock, the robot catalog, the detail card. The eye goes from the mark down the left edge, so a
  way back top left is on that path. Top centre sat over the 3D, where nothing else is read.
- **One place for both kinds of page.** The 2D pages read left to right from the mark: the mark, then the way back,
  then the page's title. Top left is the one spot that works for the world and for the pages.
- **The phone already had it there.** On a phone the brand is hidden and the bar was already top left.

**The look:** the world's way-back button on every page: mono capitals, a squared teal box, a faint fill. On the
paper Results page it takes the paper accent. It is a real `<button>` in the world and a real link on the pages,
labelled in words (the arrow is `aria-hidden`), with a visible focus ring and 44 px on touch screens.

**No Escape on the 2D pages.** There Escape belongs to the page: Record's keyboard drive and its dialogs, a picked
robot on `/robots`. Leaving a page on Escape would lose work.

## What the user can check by hand

1. **Load the site and don't scroll.** Click MACHINE in the journey rail on the right.
   - The machine plays in the lit room.
   - "← Back to room" is top left, under SANT. It lands in the room view, with the four tags and the hub bar.
   - Do the same with WORKBENCH and ROBOTS, and again from the descent.
2. **In the machine, press the browser's Back** (or swipe back on a phone).
   - The machine closes to the room, and you are still on the site. Forward opens it again.
   - Reload in the machine: it comes back open.
3. **Machine → Into the projection.** Record opens.
   - "← BACK TO WORKSHOP" is right after SANT, top left. It takes you back to the machine.
   - Then Esc (or Back to room) takes you to the room.
4. **In a new tab, open `/pipeline`, `/robots` or `/site/results`.** Click "← Back to workshop": you land in the room
   view, not at the start of the story.
5. **Shelf → a cartridge's detail.** Top left shows "← Back to shelf" and "← Back to room". Esc steps back one level
   at a time.
6. **On a phone:**
   - The way back is top left with big buttons.
   - Record's top strip no longer overlaps itself.
   - Contact sits under the bar.

## Questions for the user

1. **"Skip the intro" is keyboard-only.** It shows on Tab focus. Mouse users "skip" through the rail. Should it be a
   visible button?
2. **Should the shelf and a cartridge's detail be in the browser history too?** Then a phone's back gesture would
   close them.
3. **The projection hands off to Record on its own** after the dolly, and Plug in carries on into it. Is that what
   you want, or should it stop at the projection and wait for "Open SO-101"?

   resume where you were?
