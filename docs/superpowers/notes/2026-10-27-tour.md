# 2026-10-27: the first-visit tour (pm/tour)

The user: "we need to make a tutorial to teach someone how to use the app." Goal: a judge opening the site
understands in about 30 seconds what SANT is and where to click.

## What it is

Four short cards in the workshop's room view. Each one points at the real thing there (a ring round its tag, and a
line from the card):

| Step | Title | Points at | Says |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | What SANT does | nothing (centred card) | 10 sloppy mouse demos → 30/40 on unseen scenes; copying them directly 0/40; SANT checks, multiplies, teaches |
| 2 | Record a demo | the shelf (01, "start here") | pick a cartridge, drop it on the machine, that opens Record; link: Open Record |
| 3 | SANT checks and multiplies it | the machine (02) | replays the demo in physics, fixes or drops bad grabs, copies good ones into new scenes |
| 4 | Here's the proof | the proof wall (05) | planned first, run once, 30/40; the test track replays all 40 real runs; links: proof wall, test track |

Every number comes from `web/src/site/results/proofData.ts` (`LEAD.demos`, `LEAD.result`, `CLIMB` raw); none is typed in
the guide. A phone (or the stills, without WebGL) has the hub bar's buttons instead of tags: the ring goes round
those.

Also on `/record`: a "How to use it" strip in the camera pane (pick an input; Start, then Record; drive to the cube,
grab it, drop it in the bin; Stop: press Record again, SANT checks it in physics). It is open on a first visit, folds
with Hide or the "? How to" toggle in the pane's header, and the browser remembers which.

## Where it starts, and why

- **By itself:** the first time the visitor stands in the room view (after the story, or back from Record once the
  machine is closed). That is where all four targets are on screen, and it is the first moment the site asks for clicks.
  The room view already holds the page still, so the cards stop nothing.
- **From the hero:** a small offer, bottom left ("New here? A 30-second tour shows you where to click." Start the tour /
  No thanks), only at the top of the story. Start skips the story to the room and opens step 1. No thanks hides it
  and the tour won't start by itself (the "?" still replays it). It scrolls away with the story, and a wheel over it
  scrolls the page.
- **Never over the story:** the story is the scroll, and a card there would sit on the words it is telling.
- **"? Tour"** beside Contact (top right; on a phone under the hub bar) replays it from anywhere; away from the room it
  goes there first.
- **Once per browser:** `localStorage["sant.guide.seen"]`, set when the guide first shows (or on No thanks). Every read
  and write is in try/catch; if storage is blocked it is remembered for that page only.
- **Ends** on Skip, Done, Escape, or when the visitor leaves the room view (opens a place, the shelf, ↑ Story).

## Keys, phones, motion

- Esc skips; → / ← step (captured first, so the shelf and the dock don't also get them; ignored while typing in Ask SANT).
- 390 px: the card docks full width at the bottom (or the top when the target is low), 16 px gutters, no sideways
  scroll; the keys line is hidden on touch screens.
- `prefers-reduced-motion` (and the site's own reduced mode): no slide-in, no ring pulse; the first card doesn't wait
  for the tags' fade.

## The click-through bug class

The overlay layers are `pointer-events: none`. Every guide control has its own `pointer-events: auto`
(`guide.css`), and `web/src/world/guide/guideClicks.test.ts` asserts it for the card, the offer, "? Tour", and every
button and link class; the full-screen layer, ring and line stay `none` so the room still takes its clicks.

Real clicks: `web/scripts/guide-clicks.mjs` drives headless Chrome (SwiftShader) with CDP `Input.dispatchMouseEvent`
and checks `document.elementFromPoint` lands on each control before clicking (Start the tour, Next ×3, ? Tour, Skip,
↑ Story over the card). 36/36 pass at 1280×720 and at 390×844 on `?quality=low`; also checked on `?quality=high`
(the holographic labels) and on the stills with reduced motion (the auto-start run).

```
npm --prefix web run dev -- --port 5199
node web/scripts/guide-clicks.mjs http://localhost:5199              # SZ=390x844, Q=high|stills, REDUCED=1, OUT=dir
```

## For the user to test (about 3 minutes)

Open the site in a **private window** (a first visit), or clear `sant.guide.seen` in DevTools → Application →
Local Storage.

- [ ] The hero shows the small "New here?" box bottom left. Scrolling still works with it there, and it goes away as the story moves.
- [ ] Reload, click **Start the tour**: it jumps to the workshop and card 1 appears with 10 → 30/40 and 0/40.
- [ ] **Next** with the mouse: card 2 rings the shelf's tag, card 3 the machine's, card 4 the proof wall's.
- [ ] On card 4, **Back** works, and **Done** closes it.
- [ ] **→ / ←** step, **Esc** closes, and Esc doesn't also do something else in the room.
- [ ] **? Tour** (top right) brings it back at step 1. **Skip** closes it.
- [ ] Reload: no offer and no tour by itself any more.
- [ ] New private window, scroll the whole story down instead: the tour starts by itself when the room comes up.
- [ ] Links: "Open Record" goes to /record, "Watch the test track" to /test-track, "Open the proof wall" opens the wall.
- [ ] On a phone (or DevTools at 390 px): the card sits at the bottom, nothing scrolls sideways, all buttons tap.
- [ ] `/record`: the "How to use it" strip shows; **Hide** folds it; reload keeps it folded; **? How to** brings it back.
