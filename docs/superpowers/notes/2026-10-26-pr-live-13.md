## What's in it

### The big test's code (`pm/bigtest` `c6bae0a`, merged)
- **`sim_eval/bigtest.py` + `sim_eval/scripts/bigtest.py`:** the whole experiment as one restartable command.
  - The stages: human+teacher → Stage A → Stage B → test.
  - Each stage is skipped once it's done, and a stopped training resumes in its own folder.
- **`--gripper-deadband D`:** the RL teacher's gripper hysteresis on every student action. 0.25 means closed below
  15 mm, open above 25 mm, otherwise hold, starting open at each episode.
- **`MAX_STEPS` 200k.**
- **Seed guards for the new selection and test blocks.** The data check refuses 5020–5059, 5400–5439, 5500–5557 and
  the retired 5300–5399.
- **`lerobot_tools.experiments --resume-run`.**
- **An `nvidia-smi` log during training.**
- **DAgger rollouts** load the teacher with `cap_from_tool`.
- **Tests:** full suite on `c6bae0a`: 2,783 passed, 35 skipped.

### The held human re-execution (`pm/held-human` `ac6a8c1`, merged; off by default)
- **What it does:** `sim_eval/held_human.py` and its rollout script replay the human demos through the student's held
  executor, so both data sources share one aiming habit.
- **What happened:** it failed (0/110, then a pilot at 0/22) and was dropped by the pre-registered floor.
- **Why it's kept:** with its write-up, so the negative result is reproducible.
- **The root cause:** the held executor closes only about 9% of the gap per step.

## What it does not show

- **Real hardware:** everything is in simulation.
- **Which of v2's two changes did it:** longer training and the deadband were one combined arm.
- **DAgger's effect:** Stage B fell after a mid-training stop and resume (16 → 7–8 of 29). The training loss is
  continuous across the resume, so the cause is unknown. Its result says nothing reliable about DAgger.
- **Seed comparisons:** v2's seeds differ from v1's, so the v1 → v2 comparison is descriptive.
