# The machine, v2: a realistic GPU-style card (`pm/machine-v2`, 2026-10-20)

**The task:** the user said the machine isn't realistic. The lead modelled a GPU-style card in Blender
(`docs/design/blender/machine-v2.blend`, exported as `machine_v2.glb`: 13,952 triangles, one mesh, nine
materials, in the same frame as the old `machine.glb`). This branch puts it on the desk in place of the old GPU
parts. The desk, the hologram, the cable and the power plug stay. Fans turn, and the plug-in seats the cartridge in
the new dock.

**Branch:** from `pm/live-12` d5c2ca5. No GPU: SwiftShader screenshots only. Nothing pushed, merged or rebased.

Screenshots are in [2026-10-20-machine-v2/](2026-10-20-machine-v2/), all 1280×720.

### Baked or PBR: PBR, drawn live

**Why live PBR:**
- The card is metal: graphite (metalness 0.85), brushed aluminium, copper, gold and the fins. Metal reads by its
  reflections, which move with the camera, and a baked atlas holds only one view's light: the old baked shroud
  looked flat.
- The machine view comes close, past the dock to the projection.
- The cartridges and the robot wall are already live in the baked room, so the card sits with them.
- Re-baking would also need Blender GPU time and a new atlas.

**How it renders by tier:**
- High and ultra: a standard material with the room's small environment for reflections, as the other live parts
  have.
- Low: Lambert with the colours, no reflections (as `liveMaterial` does for every live part).

### The split (`splitMachineV2`), and the draw calls

The model is one mesh. It's split into:
- **the body:** every non-glowing material merged into one mesh. The colour, metalness and roughness go per vertex
  (`color`, `aMR`, read by `vertexPbrMaterial`).
- **the glow:** the teal strip along the front and the dock's four marks.
- **the slot:** the dock's light. It flashes as a cartridge seats, as the old slot did.
- **the lens:** the projector's glass. It lights with the projection.
- **two fans:** under a group on each axis, blades and hub as one mesh, the teal hub mark as another.

**How the fans are found in the single mesh:**
- The axes come from the teal hub marks (flat discs, 188 triangles).
- A fan is every piece of the shroud's, the hub's and the mark's materials that lies wholly within 1 m of its axis,
  and no more than 0.35 m below its mark.
- That gives exactly 11 blades of 52 triangles, the 572-triangle hub and the mark per fan (tested).
- The blade tips reach 0.957 m. The height limit keeps out the backplate's vent plates, which sit under the opening.
- So no separate export of the fans was needed.

**Fans:** they turn at 0.3 turns a second (they were 0.9), in opposite directions, on high and ultra while the
lamps are on. They don't turn on low, as before. In the page, fan 0 went from 43.7 to 51.1 rad in 4 s, which is
0.3 turns a second.

**Draw calls: unchanged.**

| | Old | New |
|---|---|---|
| Live machine | fan rings 1, strip 1, slot 1, lens 1, 2 fans × (blades + mark) 4 = **8** | body 1, glow 1, slot 1, lens 1, 2 fans × (blades + mark) 4 = **8** |

The old column is counted from `machine.glb`'s materials as the old `Scene.tsx` merged them. The machine view,

### The baked body (`cutBakedBody`)

**The problem:** the room's baked `machine_body.glb` is one mesh holding the whole old card plus its cable and plug.

**The cut:**
- In the workshop, `<Baked name="machine_body" cut={cutBakedBody}>` keeps only the pieces whose centre lies inside
  `machine.glb`'s `M_Cable` or `M_PowerPlug` box.
- That leaves 864 + 108 = 972 of its 4,368 triangles; measured in the page too.
- The test checks the boxes against the old file's nodes, and checks that nothing else survives.
- The baked file itself is unchanged, because the descent builds its particles from it (see Open).

### The plug-in

**The new dock's slot:**
- `PORT_LOCAL` is now its centre, (−3.265, 1.741). It was (−3.267, 1.737), within 5 mm.
- `SLOT_TOP_LOCAL` is 4.95 (was 4.91), which raises the seated cartridge by 4 cm.
- The test pins both to the model: the slot light's centre within 1 cm, and its top within 3 cm.

**The path:**
- The path test's furniture is now the new dock (top 4.38 m in world) and the lens barrel.
- Every slot's flight clears them: 7 tests pass.

**The hologram** stands where it stood: 0.07 m over the new lens's top, 3.79 m in the machine's frame (tested).
