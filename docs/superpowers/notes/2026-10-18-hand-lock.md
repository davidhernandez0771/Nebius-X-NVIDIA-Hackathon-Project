# Hand lock (live test #9 flicker): status, 2026-10-18

Branch `pm/hand-lock`, cut from `pm/web-record` (`4d3f3b4`) in `C:\dev\pm-t4`. Not pushed, merged or rebased.

## Why

Live test #9 showed a slight flicker at times. MediaPipe runs with `numHands: 2`, and the Record session picked "the Right label, else the first hand" afresh on every frame, both in `useRecordSession` and again inside `TeleopClient`. So a second hand in view, a label swap or a reordered detection array could hand control to the other hand for a frame.

## What changed (web only, no GPU)

- **`web/src/record/teleop/handLock.ts`**: `HandLock`, pure and frame-driven like `resetGesture.ts`.
  - **Lock.** The first hand (with a handedness label) whose palm stays within 0.05 frame heights for **0.3 s** gets locked. Its label and palm position are remembered.
  - **Follow.** Each frame only a detection with **the same label**, the **nearest to the last palm position**, counts, and only within the allowed jump. Every other hand is ignored, wherever it is and however close to the camera.
  - **Allowed jump.** 0.12 frame heights per frame at 30 fps, scaled for skipped frames, never above 0.25.
  - **Glitch.** If the locked hand's palm jumps further than that in a frame, that frame is held (`held: "glitch"`).
  - **Loss.** The last pose is held, the same landmarks and the same gripper, for up to **0.7 s** (`held: "missing"`). A hand that reappears near the last position is locked again at once.
  - **Longer loss** (`lost`). No hand drives the arm. The label is kept: only a hand with that label, held still 0.3 s again, locks. The other hand never does.
  - **Re-pick.** `reset()` drops the lock. Then the next hand held still 0.3 s locks, whichever it is.
- **`useRecordSession.ts`**: the lock replaces the per-frame pick.
  - **Downstream sees only the locked hand.** Only the locked hand, or its held copy, reaches the pose message, the link (`TeleopClient` gets a frame holding just that hand), the pinch, the V sign, the reach and the canvas.
  - **Held frames keep the arm on its last pose.** They are sent with `tracking_ok: true`, so the sim does not pause.
  - **Held frames are not new evidence.** The pinch latch gets no gap, so **the gripper stays as it was**. The reset gesture, the reach centering and Calibrate by hand skip them.
  - **The V reset gesture also re-picks the hand** (not while recording). A V kept up relocks in ~0.3 s, and it cannot fire twice: that gap is shorter than the gesture's 500 ms release.
  - **New on the session:** `pose.lock` (the status) and `repickHand()`.
- **Lock chip.**
  - **On the stage canvas** (`stageDraw.ts`), top-left: "locked: right hand", "holding: right hand", "lost: show your right hand" or "hold one hand still", with a progress bar while searching. It shows on both Record pages without touching a page component.
  - **`web/src/record/HandLockChip.tsx`** is a drop-in chip with a **Re-pick** button, for the page owner (T6) to place. One line: `<HandLockChip status={pose?.lock ?? null} onRepick={session.repickHand} />`.
- **Debug trace v4** (`debugTrace.ts`): every frame now records **every detection**, as label plus palm center, along with the lock's phase, label and held reason. A trace from the next live test is therefore a real two-hand fixture.

## What to try in the next live test

1. **Start the camera with one hand up and still.** The canvas chip should read "hold one hand still", fill its bar, and after ~0.3 s say "locked: right hand" (or left).
   - **If it names the wrong hand,** MediaPipe's label is not mirrored on this camera after all. Flip `userHand` in `handLock.ts`. It is display only; the lock itself doesn't care.
2. **Raise the other hand next to it, also closer to the camera.** The arm must not move, and the dot and skeleton stay on the locked hand. Wave the other hand, and cross it behind and in front.
3. **Pull the locked hand out of view for under a second, then bring it back near where it left.** The chip shows "holding" and the arm freezes with the gripper as it was, also while holding the cube. It resumes at once.
4. **Pull it out for 2 s or more.** The chip shows "lost: show your … hand", and the other hand still moves nothing. The same hand back and still for 0.3 s relocks.
5. **Re-pick.** Hold the V sign 1.5 s (with no recording on). This resets the scene and drops the lock; the hand held still next gets locked. To switch hands, lower the old one first. The Re-pick button appears once T6 places `HandLockChip`.
6. **Record a debug trace** with both hands in view and send me the file. It now logs every detection, and it becomes a real fixture for `handLock.test.ts`.
7. **Watch for:**
   - **Fast moves that freeze for a frame.** That would mean the jump limit of 0.12 frame heights per frame is too tight.
   - **A hand that's slow to lock** while moving gently, which would mean 0.05 steadiness is too strict.
   - Both numbers sit in `HAND_LOCK_DEFAULTS`.
